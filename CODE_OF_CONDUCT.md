# Code of conduct

Treat contributors respectfully. Welcome questions, explain disagreements with
evidence, and give constructive feedback. Respect people's privacy and backgrounds.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, doxxing, and
deliberate disruption are unacceptable in issues, pull requests, and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban
participants depending on severity and repetition. Reports are handled with
discretion; information is shared only as needed to investigate and respond.

Use GitHub's **Report content** feature on the affected issue, comment, or profile
for private reports to GitHub. Maintainer: [Jerrett Davis](https://github.com/JerrettDavis).
Do not publish private information in an issue.
