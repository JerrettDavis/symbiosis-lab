FIELD GUIDE

Watch mechanisms, not just patterns.

Each cell reads nearby food, waste, and chemical signals. A seven-input, seven-output controller regulates uptake, repair, division, emissions, movement, and sharing. Its 49 weights and 11 traits are copied, with possible mutation, when it divides.

LOCAL FIELDS → RECEPTORS → CONTROLLER → ACTIONS → LOCAL FIELDS

Three different kinds of change

Homeostasis: cells spend energy to maintain integrity. Adaptation: receptor gains change within a lifetime. Evolution: descendants inherit mutations and experience differential survival and reproduction. The controller topology itself does not evolve in this release.

Try a controlled intervention

Pause and save a checkpoint. Select a cell, switch to Channel A, and apply a Pulse A nearby. Advance 100 ticks and inspect its receptor gains and controller inputs. Restore the checkpoint and repeat with Chemical signaling switched off. The headless experiment command supports matched-seed runs across multiple seeds.

Read the visualization carefully

Colors track founder ancestry, not species. Lines show physical adjacency, not measured message traffic. A/B fields are abstract diffusing chemicals, not electrical nerves or a literal digital bus. Connected patches do not establish multicellular individuality.

What is deliberately supplied?

The initial cells, local decision scaffold, cell boundaries, two signal channels, reproduction machinery, and external resource source are designed. There is no spontaneous origin of life, learned morphogenesis, proven cooperation, or consciousness detector here. Cells never execute arbitrary code or contact the outside world.

Persistence

JSON checkpoints preserve the world, cells, histories, and random-generator state. Imports and restores pause the simulation. Replay is exact on the same runtime and model version; UI layer and playback speed are not biological state. Changing a setting alone is not evidence of improved fitness.
